The Consignment Grimoire

Three simple steps to sell your storied pieces

✉
I

Submit Your Wares

Send us photos and a description of your piece via our contact form. We accept garments, accessories, props, armor, and handcrafted goods tied to renaissance, LARP, or fantasy themes.

➤
🔍
II

We Curate & List

Our team reviews each item for quality and theme. Approved pieces are photographed, priced collaboratively with you, and listed in the Arcane Market within 7 days.

➤
🎁
III

Earn Your Gold

When your item sells, you receive 70% of the sale price. We handle payments, shipping coordination, and buyer communication so you can focus on creating.

Our Story

A Market Born of Passion

Made by Mages began at a renaissance faire when two friends realized that the most beautiful costumes and props often end up forgotten in storage bins after the event. Too much love went into these pieces to let them gather dust.

We built a curated marketplace where artisans, costumers, LARPers, and fantasy enthusiasts can give their treasured items a second life — and where buyers can find one-of-a-kind pieces that carry real stories within their seams.

Every item in our market has been handled by human hands, worn in enchanted fields, and imagined in worlds beyond the ordinary. We believe that is worth something.
